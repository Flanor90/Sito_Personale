Who writes them

I read Clinical Psychology at Sapienza University of Rome and have been a licensed psychologist since 2018. In 2022 I completed the four-year specialisation in Psychoanalytic Psychotherapy at SPS in Rome, training in Demand Analysis: an approach that is not satisfied with the symptom, but asks what the request for help means inside the history and the contexts of the person who brings it.

Alongside clinical practice I have worked on the organisational side — the distress that comes from work, and the organisations that produce it. That double vantage point runs through everything I write: suffering never lives in a vacuum, it lives inside relationships, contexts and cultures.

I see clients online and in person in Itri, near Rome, and I work in Italian, English and Spanish.

Questions

Are the compendia written for clinicians or for patients?

For clinicians. They assume you already sit with patients and take for granted the vocabulary of the consulting room. Two of them are also readable by an informed non-specialist, but the address is professional throughout.

What format are they, and how are they delivered?

PDF, and PDF plus EPUB for the longer two. Delivery is an instant download from Etsy the moment you buy: nothing is shipped and nothing arrives later by email.

Are these translations of your Italian work?

No. They are standalone English texts, written for colleagues who work in English. The Italian editions cover different ground and live on Gumroad.

Do you offer clinical supervision in English?

Yes, online, individually or in small groups. I work in Italian, English and Spanish. Writing to me is the fastest way to see whether it makes sense for the case or the team you have in mind.
