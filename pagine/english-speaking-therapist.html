Psychotherapy in English

An English-speaking psychotherapist, online or in Italy

Online by video call from anywhere, or in person in my practice in Itri, between Rome and Naples. We start with a 45-minute introductory session.

Looking for a therapist in English is often harder than it should be. If you live in Italy, most of the people you find work only in Italian. If you live elsewhere, you may be looking for someone outside the system you are in, or simply someone who thinks about suffering in a particular way.

This page is for you if you want to talk about what is happening to you, in English, with a psychotherapist who is properly qualified and who will take the time to understand your question before offering answers.

Who I work with

I see adults and couples. People usually come with something that has been weighing on them for a while, and that they have not managed to shift on their own.

  • anxiety and panic attacks, including the kind that seems to come from nowhere;
  • low mood and depression, when everything feels switched off;
  • burnout and work stress, when rest no longer helps;
  • relationships that keep repeating the same scene, alone or as a couple;
  • the particular fatigue of living abroad: distance, loneliness, a life rebuilt in another language.

You do not need a diagnosis to start. Many people arrive with a discomfort that has no name yet, and that is often a good place to begin.

How I work

My orientation is psychoanalytic. In practice this means that I do not start from a protocol to apply, but from what you bring: the words you use, the moments when the problem appears, what it protects you from as well as what it costs you.

My training is in the Analysis of the Demand, a way of working that pays close attention to the question behind the request. People often come asking for one thing, for instance to stop having panic attacks, and along the way discover that the question is also about something else. Taking that seriously is usually what makes the change last.

It is not a lecture and it is not a chat. It is a space with a few clear rules, regular times and a person who listens closely, where you can say things you would not say elsewhere.

Online, or in Itri

Most of my English-speaking clients see me online, by video call. It works well for therapy, as long as you have a quiet room where nobody can hear you and a stable connection. We find times that work across time zones.

If you live in southern Lazio or spend time there, you can also see me in person, in my practice in Itri, a few minutes from Gaeta, Formia, Sperlonga and Fondi.

A word about language

I am Italian. English is a language I have worked in for years, including in clinical roles at international online therapy services, but it is not my mother tongue, and I prefer to say so openly.

Language matters in therapy, and I have written about it at length. If at some point you notice that something gets lost between us, we can talk about it. That conversation is rarely a waste of time: what is hard to put into words is often exactly what we are looking for.

About me

I am a psychologist and psychotherapist, registered with the Order of Psychologists of Lazio (no. 23754). I studied at Sapienza University of Rome and specialised in psychoanalytic psychotherapy at SPS in Rome.

Alongside my clinical work I spent several years in digital mental health, as Clinical Team Lead at Unobravo and in BetterHelp's European clinical operations. Everything you tell me is covered by professional confidentiality under Italian law.

How to start

We begin with a 45-minute introductory session. We try to understand together what is happening and whether working together could help. At the end you decide whether and how to continue, with no commitment. To book it, a short message is enough: I reply myself, usually within a day.
